data_preprocessing

Configurable preprocessing pipeline and outlier clipping for sensor data.

The DataPreprocessor is an sklearn-style Pipeline assembled from configurable DataTransformer steps (see DataPreprocessor.STEP_REGISTRY). The pipeline is configured via the train.data_preprocessor.steps config key.
